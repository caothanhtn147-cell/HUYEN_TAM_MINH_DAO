# HUYỀN TÂM MINH ĐẠO (HuyenTam Wisdom)

> **Vietnamese Slogan:** “Thấy rõ sự thật – Hiểu mình – Sống tốt hơn”  
> **English Slogan:** “See Clearly. Live Wisely.”  
> **AI Guide:** Minh Sư AI  

---

## 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW & PURPOSE

HUYỀN TÂM MINH ĐẠO is a multilingual paid web platform combining AI-assisted spiritual self-reflection, honest personal guidance, Eastern and Western philosophy, daily lifestyle education, traditional wellness knowledge (Nam Y history), and personal development roadmaps.

The platform recreates the emotional privacy, symbolic depth, and personal focus of a traditional consultation while guided by the core personality of **Compassionate Candor** (*Thẳng Thắn Có Lòng Từ*).

---

## 2. EXPLICIT AI DISCLOSURE & SAFETY BOUNDARIES

1. **AI Content Disclosure**: All advice, interpretations, Tarot views, and consultations on this platform are generated by AI systems.
2. **Zero Supernatural Claims**: The platform MUST NEVER claim supernatural powers, spirit communication, magic, or divine authority.
3. **No Fatalistic Predictions**: Tarot and symbolic reflections are framed strictly as psychological mirrors and intuitive metaphors—never as guaranteed predictions or unchangeable destiny.
4. **Non-Clinical Health Boundary**: The Dưỡng Đạo section is strictly educational. It does not provide medical care, diagnoses, or prescriptions.
5. **Crisis Protocol**: If acute crisis indicators (self-harm, medical emergency) are detected, spiritual AI interpretation is immediately suspended and emergency hotline contacts are provided.
