# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD) — HUYỀN TÂM MINH ĐẠO

**International Name:** HuyenTam Wisdom  
**AI Guide:** Minh Sư AI  
**Vietnamese Slogan:** “Thấy rõ sự thật – Hiểu mình – Sống tốt hơn”  
**English Slogan:** “See Clearly. Live Wisely.”  

---

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

HUYỀN TÂM MINH ĐẠO is a premium, multilingual paid web platform that combines AI-assisted spiritual self-reflection, honest life guidance, traditional Asian wellness wisdom, personal philosophy, and daily lifestyle education.

The platform recreates the emotional privacy, symbolic depth, and personalized focus of a traditional consultation while maintaining strict transparency that all output is generated by AI. Guided by the core personality of **Compassionate Candor** (*Thẳng Thắn Có Lòng Từ*), the AI guide (**Minh Sư AI**) provides realistic, grounded perspectives rather than superficial flattery or fear-inducing predictions.

---

## 2. TARGET USERS & USER PROBLEMS

### 2.1 Target Audiences
1. **Young Urban Professionals (Ages 22–38)**: Facing career burnout, relationship uncertainties, financial habits, stress, and existential identity questions.
2. **Older Adults & Traditional Vietnamese (Ages 39–65+)**: Interested in traditional symbolism, Tử Vi, Bát Tự, I Ching, seasonal health routines, and family harmony.
3. **Overseas Vietnamese (Viet Kieu)**: Seeking connection to cultural heritage, Eastern philosophy, and spiritual reflection in English or bilingual Vietnamese.
4. **International Seekers**: Global users fascinated by Asian philosophy, Eastern mindfulness, non-dogmatic spiritual reflection, and traditional lifestyle balance.

### 2.2 Core User Problems Solved
- **Overcoming Fear-Based Superstition**: Traditional fortune-telling often uses fear manipulation ("you have a curse", "disaster will strike unless you pay for a ritual"). HUYỀN TÂM MINH ĐẠO provides honest, non-superstitious reflection.
- **Lack of Emotional Privacy**: Users are hesitant to share vulnerable personal struggles (grief, relationship failure, debt) with friends or family.
- **Unrealistic Positivity vs. Harsh Judgment**: Popular self-help often gives empty positivity ("everything happens for a reason"), while traditional advisors can be overly fatalistic. Minh Sư AI delivers balanced, actionable truth.
- **Confusion Between Health Advice & Superstition**: Distinguishes daily lifestyle education from medical treatment.

## 3. PRODUCT AREAS

The platform is structured into two main public areas:

```
                      HUYỀN TÂM MINH ĐẠO
                               │
         ┌─────────────────────┴─────────────────────┐
         ▼                                           ▼
  MINH ĐẠO (Spiritual & Life Guidance)     DƯỠNG ĐẠO (Lifestyle & Wellness)
  - AI Life Consultation (Minh Kiến)        - Daily Lifestyle Education
  - Tarot & Symbolic Reflection            - Sleep, Rest & Hydration Routines
  - I Ching & Eastern Philosophy           - Traditional Wellness History
  - Dream Interpretation                    - Seasonal Health Rhythms
  - Personalized Reports (Lá Thư)          - Pre-Doctor Visit Preparation
```

### 3.1 MINH ĐẠO (Spiritual Reflection & Life Guidance)
- **Minh Kiến Sessions**: Deep, structured AI consultations addressing relationships, career, family, money habits, grief, loneliness, and motivation.
- **Tarot & Symbolic Reflection**: Non-predictive Tarot card draws framed as psychological mirrors and intuitive introspection.
- **Eastern Philosophical Reflection**: Insights drawn from I Ching, Tử Vi concepts, Bát Tự elements, and Vietnamese folk philosophy presented strictly as symbolic frameworks.
- **Lá Thư Huyền Tâm**: Comprehensive AI-generated personalized reports summarizing long-term self-reflection, life roadmaps, and boundary setting.

### 3.2 DƯỠNG ĐẠO (Educational Lifestyle & Wellness)
- **Daily Lifestyle Education**: Non-clinical guidance on sleep hygiene, physical movement, stress management, hydration, and rest-work balance.
- **Traditional Wellness History**: Historical education on Nam Y (Vietnamese traditional medicine), Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) philosophy, Ayurveda, and Ancient Greek wellness principles.
- **Seasonal & Daily Routines**: Natural daily rhythm suggestions aligned with seasonal changes.
- **Healthcare Preparation**: Helping users formulate clear questions for licensed medical professionals.

## 5. MONETIZATION MODEL

1. **Internal Credit Wallet ("Linh Điểm")**:
   - Pay-per-session model using top-up packages of Linh Điểm.
   - Transparent credit deduct per consultation type (e.g., Minh Kiến session = 10 Linh Điểm, Personalized Report = 25 Linh Điểm).
2. **One-Time Premium Reports**:
   - Custom synthesized reports ("Lá Thư Huyền Tâm") generated as downloadable PDFs or interactive web views.
3. **Membership Tiers (Future)**:
   - Monthly/Annual passes granting daily reflection allowances and bonus credits.
4. **Optional Gratitude Tips**:
   - Optional tipping function after a helpful session. **Strict Rule**: Tips have ZERO influence on consultation outcome or AI algorithms.

## 6. NON-FUNCTIONAL & SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

### 6.1 Localization & Accessibility
- Full bilingual UI (Vietnamese, English) powered by `next-intl`.
- Responsive web layout optimized for mobile browsers (where 80%+ of Vietnamese users access web services).
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance (high contrast text, screen reader compatibility).

### 6.2 Privacy & Security
- End-to-end HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.
- Birth date/time data encrypted at rest (AES-256).
- Zero retention of prompts in analytics logs; complete log PII redaction.
- Account deletion ("Right to be Forgotten") with clean anonymization of financial audit logs.

### 6.3 Safety & Compliance
- Automated safety reviewer inspecting all AI prompt outputs before display.
- Immediate suspension of spiritual guidance when crisis signals (self-harm, acute medical symptoms) are detected.
- Explicit disclaimers on every page confirming content is AI-generated and educational.

---

## 7. EXPLICIT EXCLUSIONS

The platform MUST explicitly prohibit and exclude:
- Claims of supernatural powers, spirit communication, or magic spells.
- Medical diagnosis, psychiatric diagnosis, or drug/herb prescriptions.
- Financial or investment advice.
- Guaranteed predictions of marriage, lottery, death, or disaster.
- Fear-based monetization or spiritual protection fees.
