## 1. DƯỠNG ĐẠO MISSION & EDUCATIONAL SCOPE

**Dưỡng Đạo** is the lifestyle education and traditional wellness arm of HUYỀN TÂM MINH ĐẠO. Its mission is to educate users on daily habits, sleep hygiene, movement, stress reduction, and the rich cultural history of traditional Eastern and Western wellness philosophies.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Dưỡng Đạo is strictly educational and non-clinical. It does not provide medical care, telemedicine, medical diagnosis, or pharmaceutical/herbal prescriptions.

---

## 2. ALLOWED VS. DISALLOWED CONTENT MATRIX

| Category | Allowed Educational Content | Strictly Disallowed Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily Lifestyle** | Sleep routines, hydration habits, work-rest balance, breathing exercises, ergonomics. | Diagnosing insomnia, prescribing sleep medications or sedatives. |
| **Traditional Wellness History** | Historical background of Nam Y (Vietnamese Traditional Wellness), TCM philosophy, Ayurveda, Ancient Greek humors. | Stating that traditional herbs cure cancer, diabetes, viral infections, or chronic diseases. |
| **Seasonal Rhythms** | Cultural dietary habits, seasonal tea traditions, weather-aligned rest patterns. | Recommending specific medicinal dosages, herbal decoctions, or chemical supplements. |
| **Healthcare Prep** | Helping users organize symptoms to communicate clearly with their doctors. | Evaluating lab tests, prescribing blood panels, or telling users to skip doctor appointments. |

---

## 3. HERB, SUPPLEMENT & PRESCRIPTION BOUNDARIES

1. **Zero Herbal Prescriptions**: Dưỡng Đạo must never give specific herbal formulas (e.g., "Boil 10g of Tuoi Sam with 5g of Dang Shen").
2. **Zero Dosage Recommendations**: No advice on milligram dosages for vitamins, minerals, or traditional remedies.
3. **Medication Continuity**: If a user mentions taking prescribed drugs (e.g., blood pressure medication), Dưỡng Đạo must ALWAYS remind them to follow their licensed physician's instructions without alteration.
